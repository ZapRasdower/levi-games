Levi grabs his shovel and digs straight down through real layers of rock. Collect ores and gems to sell, dig up fossils and treasures for the museum, and try to reach the legendary Crystal Cave, 500 m down.

THE CRYSTAL CAVE!

Levi dug 500 metres down and found a giant crystal — taller than a house!

Real giant crystals like this grow in Mexico's Naica Cave, up to 12 m long. They grew for half a million years in hot, mineral-rich water.

And Earth's crust here would be about 35 km thick — so Levi dug about 1.4% of the way through it. The planet is BIG!
