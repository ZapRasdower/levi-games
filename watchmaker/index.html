HOW A BUILD WORKS
0. Choose a watch from history and a grade of craftsmanship
1. Hand-finish the plate, build and oil the movement, then case it — every part in its proper order
2. Tap each part's glowing slot — closer to the bullseye, more points
3. Seat it: stop the needle in the gold zone
4. Wind the mainspring to bring it to life — don't overwind!
5. Regulate it on the timegrapher, then set it to the real time
6. Choose your finish and add the watch to your showcase
